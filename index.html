THE POST-PURCHASE GAP

The most valuable part of the product journey often happens after checkout.

After purchase, brands often know what was bought — but have little visibility into what consumers need, question, or experience while actually using the product.

  • “Am I using this correctly?”
  • “Can I use this with vitamin C?”
  • “Why does this feel different today?”

Beauty Echo helps make these post-purchase needs visible through contextual conversations, giving consumers relevant guidance while giving brands a clearer view of the product journey.

HOW IT WORKS

From purchase to personalized guidance to the next best action.

  1. 01 — Purchase

    Start the relationship when the product is purchased.

    A convenient, private channel keeps the post-purchase relationship centered on the product the consumer purchased.

  2. 02 — Personalized Guidance

    Support each consumer throughout their skincare journey.

    Consumers get caring support for questions, product use, and routines—making skincare easier and more reassuring.

  3. 03 — Next Best Action

    Turn everyday conversations into lasting brand value.

    Post-purchase interactions become structured insight that helps brands make better-informed product and engagement decisions.
