How It Works

Engage · Analyze · Recommend

Engage

Consumers who share similar concerns and interests are brought together into a space where peer-to-peer conversations naturally spark new insights.

Analyze

Emerging themes, sentiment, and ingredient trends from the conversations are identified through Natural Language Processing (NLP), uncovering deeper insights automatically.

Recommend

Insights are transformed into testable actions, creative ideas, and faster product iterations.
