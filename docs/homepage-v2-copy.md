# 3. POST-PURCHASE PROBLEM

### THE POST-PURCHASE GAP

# The most valuable part of the product journey often happens after checkout.

Brands know what a customer purchased.

But during the weeks that follow, consumers are learning how to use the product, deciding how it fits into their routine, experiencing it in real life, and forming the opinion that may shape their next purchase.

Questions emerge:

### “Am I using this correctly?”

### “Where does this fit into my routine?”

### “Can I use this with vitamin C?”

### “Why does this feel different today?”

### “What should I consider next?”

Most of these moments never become a meaningful interaction with the brand.

And most of the insight behind them is lost.

**Beauty Echo is designed to make this invisible part of the product journey visible — and useful.**
