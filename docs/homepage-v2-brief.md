# Beauty Echo Homepage V2 — Business & Design Brief

## 1. Objective

Beauty Echo Homepage V2 is a B2B company homepage designed primarily to generate conversations with prospective beauty-brand partners.

The homepage should allow a qualified beauty-industry decision maker to understand within approximately 30–60 seconds:

1. what post-purchase problem Beauty Echo addresses,
2. what Beauty Echo does and how the experience works,
3. why consumers may find the service useful,
4. what brands can learn from post-purchase consumer interactions,
5. how Beauty Echo can help brands communicate more intelligently with consumers,
6. why Beauty Echo has credible beauty-science and product-development foundations,
7. and what the visitor should do next if they are interested in working with Beauty Echo.

The homepage should communicate these points through a clear sequence of:

problem
→ solution
→ consumer experience
→ proof of usefulness
→ brand value
→ partnership opportunity

The page should not over-explain or repeatedly argue for Beauty Echo's differentiation.

Where possible, differentiation should be demonstrated implicitly through the product experience, scientific grounding, continuity of consumer context, and brand-specific communication capabilities.

The primary conversion objective is:

> Discuss a Brand Partnership

---

## 2. Positioning

Beauty Echo is the post-purchase intelligence and engagement layer between a beauty purchase and the consumer's next purchase.

Beauty Echo provides AI-powered post-purchase engagement for beauty brands.

For consumers, Beauty Echo provides contextual guidance based on:

- the product purchased,
- the consumer's routine,
- their questions,
- their evolving product experience,
- and relevant context accumulated across interactions.

For beauty brands, Beauty Echo creates two complementary forms of value:

1. deeper understanding of what happens during the post-purchase product journey,
2. a more contextual channel through which brand-approved knowledge and guidance can reach consumers.

An important internal differentiation principle remains:

> Generic AI starts from a question. Beauty Echo starts from the consumer's product journey.

However, Homepage V2 should not repeatedly state or defend this comparison.

The homepage should demonstrate differentiation primarily through what Beauty Echo actually enables.

---

## 3. What Beauty Echo is not

Homepage V2 should no longer position Beauty Echo as:

- a consumer community,
- a group-buying platform,
- a peer-to-peer beauty discussion platform,
- an NLP dashboard business,
- a generic AI skincare chatbot.

These were elements of previous concepts and should not define Homepage V2.

Beauty Echo should also not be positioned primarily as:

- an analytics dashboard,
- a generic customer-support bot,
- an advertising technology workaround,
- or a replacement for the brand's existing expertise.

Beauty Echo should instead help put brand knowledge, consumer context, and post-purchase intelligence into a more useful ongoing relationship.

## 5. Core commercial problem

Beauty companies invest heavily in customer acquisition.

Yet after checkout, much of the relationship becomes quiet during the period when the consumer is actually learning whether the product belongs in their life.

During the following weeks, consumers may be asking questions such as:

- Am I using this correctly?
- Where does this fit in my routine?
- Can I use this with another ingredient?
- Why does the texture feel different today?
- Is this product suitable for the way my skin currently feels?
- Should I continue using it this way?
- What should I consider adding next?

Most of this product-journey information never becomes structured brand knowledge.

Traditional post-purchase CRM generally knows that a purchase occurred.

It knows much less about what happens between that purchase and the consumer's next purchase decision.

Beauty Echo is designed to operate inside this post-purchase gap.

## 6. Beauty Echo Consumer Experience

Beauty Echo gives consumers access to an AI-powered beauty expert through an interaction channel such as LINE.

The experience should be presented as contextual rather than generic.

Relevant context can include:

- the product purchased,
- product/formulation context,
- the consumer's routine,
- the consumer's questions,
- product compatibility considerations,
- sensory/product experience,
- previous conversations,
- and changes across multiple days.

The Homepage V2 Consumer Experience section should focus on three representative forms of consumer value.

### Compatibility & Cosmetic Science

Beauty Echo helps consumers navigate product and ingredient questions with scientifically grounded, appropriately cautious explanations.

Representative question:

> “Can I use this with vitamin C?”

### Skin Changes Over Time

Beauty Echo encourages consumers to notice and describe small changes in their skin and product experience over time.

The purpose is not medical diagnosis or clinical skin measurement.

The value is helping consumers pay closer attention to their skin, understand the product's role more clearly, and engage more thoughtfully with their skincare routine.

Representative question:

> “Why does my skin feel different this week?”

### Personalized Journey

Beauty Echo carries forward relevant context from previous interactions so that follow-ups and check-ins can become increasingly fitted to the consumer.

The benefit should be framed as continuity, relevance, and a more natural and enjoyable product journey.

Do not imply that the underlying AI model retrains itself individually on each consumer.

Website demonstrations should remain scientifically conservative and should not make medical diagnoses or unsafe ingredient claims.

---

## 7. How Beauty Echo Works

The high-level journey should be understandable without technical expertise.

### 1. Purchase

The consumer purchases the brand's product.

Beauty Echo begins with product-specific context rather than an isolated consumer question.

### 2. Personalized Guidance

Beauty Echo understands the purchased product, the consumer's routine, questions, and evolving experience through post-purchase conversations.

### 3. Next Best Action

Beauty Echo helps the consumer navigate product use while turning relevant interaction into structured intelligence and potential next actions for the brand.

This replaces the Homepage V1 framework:

Engage
→ Analyze
→ Recommend

The How It Works section should primarily explain the process.

It does not require elaborate custom imagery if typography and information design communicate the progression clearly.

## 8. Brand Intelligence

Post-purchase conversations can create structured information concerning areas such as:

- product experience,
- product-use behavior,
- routine context,
- consumer questions,
- compatibility concerns,
- sensory experience,
- recurring friction,
- consumer motivations,
- emerging needs,
- repurchase-intent signals,
- and cross-sell opportunities.

The strategic opportunity is to understand relationships across the evolving product journey:

product context
→ routine
→ experience
→ questions
→ guidance
→ subsequent behavior

This is more valuable than merely summarizing sentiment or extracting isolated comments.

However, Brand Intelligence is an underlying strategic capability.

Homepage V2 does not need a separate technical analytics section explaining every possible data output.

The public story should be expressed primarily through the three brand capabilities defined below.

---

## 9. Value for Beauty Brands

The Homepage V2 brand-value section should communicate three distinct capabilities.

Together they represent a progression:

understand
→ discover
→ communicate

### Understand Product Use

Beauty Echo helps brands understand how consumers actually live with the product after purchase.

Relevant information may include:

- consumer questions,
- routines,
- product combinations,
- usage patterns,
- and real-world product experiences.

### Discover Consumer Insights

Beauty Echo can help transform fragmented individual conversations into recurring patterns that are useful to the brand.

Consumer insight should mean meaningful patterns and implications involving areas such as:

- recurring questions,
- consumer behaviors,
- friction points,
- motivations,
- and emerging needs.

This should not be positioned merely as:

- sentiment analysis,
- conversation summarization,
- comment collection,
- or another NLP dashboard.

### Build a Smarter Communication Channel

Beauty Echo can also function as an interactive post-purchase communication layer through which brand-approved knowledge can be delivered more contextually.

Relevant material may include:

- product knowledge,
- usage education,
- skincare guidance,
- educational priorities,
- and contextual explanations.

The strategic advantage is:

context
+ timing
+ interaction
+ personalization
+ depth

rather than simply sending more messages.

Beauty Echo must not be positioned as a way to bypass:

- advertising restrictions,
- regulatory requirements,
- cosmetic-claims rules,
- or other compliance obligations.

The public homepage should therefore communicate three different forms of brand value:

1. understand what is happening,
2. discover what can be learned,
3. communicate more intelligently.

## 10. Previous Proof of Concept Evidence

Beauty Echo previously conducted a proof of concept that generated consumer-reported evidence of usefulness.

The public homepage should refer to this as:

> Previous Proof of Concept

rather than:

> POC II

because external visitors will not understand internal phase terminology.

Final survey:

n = 16

Reported ratings:

- Product experience improvement: 4.81 / 5
- Questions solved / reassurance: 4.69 / 5
- Expertise / trust: 4.69 / 5
- Motivation to converse: 4.44 / 5

The qualitative public-facing synthesis should communicate:

> Participants reported value in four recurring areas: quick answers to everyday skincare questions, product-and-routine compatibility guidance, clear scientific explanations, and continuity across conversations.

The public homepage does not need to display a detailed commercial-outcome disclaimer directly beside these results.

However, the internal evidence boundary remains strict.

These findings demonstrate consumer-reported usefulness only.

They do NOT establish that Beauty Echo increased:

- LTV,
- repurchase,
- conversion,
- sales,
- revenue.

Do not turn the proof-of-concept results into unsupported commercial claims.

## 11. Differentiation — Internal Strategic Guidance

Homepage V2 should NOT contain a standalone "Why Beauty Echo" differentiation section.

Earlier versions proposed a dedicated section built around:

> Generic AI starts from a question. Beauty Echo starts from the consumer's product journey.

and four contextual layers:

1. Product Intelligence
2. Cosmetic Science
3. Longitudinal Consumer Memory
4. Behavioral Orchestration

These concepts remain strategically useful but should not be presented as another long standalone homepage argument.

The homepage should instead demonstrate these qualities implicitly:

- product context through How Beauty Echo Works,
- cosmetic science through Consumer Experience,
- longitudinal understanding through Skin Changes Over Time and Personalized Journey,
- brand knowledge and contextual communication through For Beauty Brands,
- relevant domain credibility through the final partnership section.

The four-layer framework and broader comparison with general-purpose AI remain useful for:

- sales conversations,
- investor materials,
- enterprise objection handling,
- technical explanations,
- future sales decks,
- FAQs.

The homepage should not repeatedly try to prove that Beauty Echo is superior to generic AI.

Do not unnecessarily introduce objections such as:

> “Why can't we just connect ChatGPT to LINE?”

The homepage should communicate differentiation primarily by showing what Beauty Echo does rather than arguing against alternatives.

## 14. Partnership and Founder Credibility

Homepage V2 should not contain a standalone founder-biography section.

Founder credibility should be integrated into the final partnership section as a concise supporting proof point.

The final section should primarily answer:

> Why should a beauty brand partner with Beauty Echo?

The partnership proposition should focus on what the brand can gain from working with Beauty Echo.

The public partnership story should NOT imply that prospective brands are being recruited primarily to:

- help Beauty Echo prove the concept,
- validate whether the business works,
- prepare Beauty Echo for its next startup stage,
- or compensate for a lack of existing customers.

Instead, the partnership proposition should communicate three benefits.

### Guide consumers with the brand's expertise

Bring brand-approved product knowledge, usage guidance, and skincare education into contextual conversations when consumers actually need them.

### Learn from real product journeys

Understand how consumers use products, what questions emerge, where friction occurs, and what ongoing experiences can reveal.

### Build more relevant ongoing engagement

Use post-purchase context to make education, CRM communication, repurchase engagement, and cross-sell opportunities more relevant to the individual consumer.

Founder credibility should then be summarized in approximately one sentence.

Approved strategic direction:

> Founded by Delong Wang, Ph.D., a University of Tokyo–trained applied chemical biologist with experience spanning commercial beauty-product development, skin-science research, and R&D leadership at major Japanese beauty companies.

This is sufficient to establish relevant domain credibility.

The homepage does not need:

- a founder résumé,
- a career timeline,
- previous employer names,
- employer logos,
- extensive founder biography,
- popularity/sales claims about products previously developed.

The founder credential exists to reinforce Beauty Echo's beauty-science and product-development foundation.

It should remain secondary to the commercial partnership proposition.

The final CTA should be:

> Discuss a Brand Partnership

## 15. Visual Direction

The visual language should communicate:

- premium beauty,
- scientific competence,
- calmness,
- sophistication,
- modern technology,
- enterprise credibility.

The site should feel closer to a premium skincare/scientific company than a generic AI SaaS startup.

Avoid:

- generic AI neon effects,
- robots,
- excessive gradients,
- excessive glassmorphism,
- excessive animation,
- visual clutter,
- generic SaaS stock illustrations,
- overly technical dashboard imagery without a clear business reason.

Do not solve excessive text density simply by adding decorative images.

First:

- remove unnecessary explanation,
- simplify concepts,
- improve information hierarchy.

Use imagery only where it materially improves comprehension.

Existing visual assets and layout patterns may be reused when they genuinely support the new story.

---

## 16. Scrolling and Layout

Homepage V2 should use natural vertical scrolling.

The mandatory desktop full-screen scroll snap used in Homepage V1 should be removed for the English V2 homepage.

Sections should be allowed to have different heights according to content.

The experience should support rapid scanning by busy beauty-industry decision makers.

The English V2 layout should remain scoped so that development does not unintentionally alter the existing Japanese V1 site before localization begins.

---

Homepage V2 should be implemented in controlled passes.

### Pass 1

- Navigation
- Hero
- Post-purchase problem
- natural vertical scrolling

### Pass 2

- How Beauty Echo Works
- Consumer Experience

### Pass 3

- Previous Proof of Concept
- For Beauty Brands

The original standalone Why Beauty Echo / differentiation section has been removed from the homepage architecture.

### Pass 4

- integrated Partner with Beauty Echo / founder-credibility section
- final commercial CTA
- footer
- removal of remaining Homepage V1 Team / Contact content

There should be no separate founder section followed by a separate partnership section.

They should form one coherent closing commercial section.

### Final Structural Review

After Pass 4, review the entire homepage before detailed visual polishing.

Evaluate:

- narrative flow,
- duplicated concepts,
- excessive text,
- unnecessary sections,
- overall hierarchy,
- whether a beauty-brand decision maker can understand the business quickly,
- whether the homepage appears commercially operable and credible,
- whether any language unnecessarily exposes internal startup-stage concerns.

### Visual Polish Pass

Only after the full structure is accepted, refine:

- typography,
- line breaks,
- spacing,
- card proportions,
- section rhythm,
- colors,
- borders,
- responsive details,
- final imagery.

Do not delay structural completion to perfect minor typography or spacing.

### Image Strategy

Do not add images merely because a section contains text.

Use custom visual effort only where imagery materially improves comprehension.

Current image priority:

1. Consumer Experience — highest priority.
2. Other sections — imagery only where clearly necessary.

The likely Consumer Experience visual strategy is a maximum of approximately three coordinated visual/UI treatments:

- compatibility consultation,
- skin observation over time,
- personalized follow-up/check-in.

These should use one coherent visual language rather than unrelated imagery.

How Beauty Echo Works, Previous Proof of Concept, For Beauty Brands, and the final Partnership section can primarily rely on strong information design.

A founder photograph is not required for Homepage V2.

If founder imagery is later considered, it should only be used if it materially improves trust and fits the overall visual system.

## 20. Success Criterion for V2

Homepage V2 does not need to be the final permanent Beauty Echo corporate website.

Its immediate purpose is to be credible, understandable, and commercially strong enough to send directly to qualified beauty-brand decision makers and initiate serious partnership conversations.

A successful visitor should leave the homepage understanding:

- the post-purchase problem Beauty Echo addresses,
- how the consumer experience works,
- why consumers may find it useful,
- what the brand can understand and learn,
- how the brand can communicate more intelligently through Beauty Echo,
- why Beauty Echo has credible beauty-science foundations,
- and how to begin a partnership conversation.

The homepage should not make Beauty Echo appear as though it is asking prospective brands to validate an unfinished idea.

It should present Beauty Echo as a focused B2B capability that brands can explore adopting.

Clarity, credibility, scientific discipline, commercial relevance, and confidence are more important than adding complexity.
